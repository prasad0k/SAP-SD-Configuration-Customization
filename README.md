# SAP SD Configuration and Customization – SAP S/4HANA

## About the Project

This project is part of my learning journey in SAP Sales and Distribution (SAP SD). The main purpose of this project is to understand how SAP SD works in a real business environment and how different sales activities are managed through SAP S/4HANA.

Through this project, I explored the configuration and customization aspects of SAP SD and learned how different settings help a business manage its daily sales operations smoothly.

I also focused on understanding how SAP SD connects with other SAP modules, especially SAP Finance (FI), to support an integrated business process.

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Understand the basic concepts and business functions of SAP SD.
- Learn how SAP SD configuration supports business requirements.
- Understand the Order-to-Cash (O2C) process.
- Explore different configuration settings used in sales and distribution.
- Understand how SAP SD works with other SAP modules.
- Identify possible enhancements and customizations based on business needs.

## Topics Covered

### 1. Enterprise Structure
Understanding the organizational structure used in SAP SD, including sales organization, distribution channel, division, sales area, plant and shipping point.

### 2. Master Data
Understanding the importance of customer master data and material master data in managing sales transactions.

### 3. Sales Order Management
Learning how sales orders are created and processed based on customer requirements.

### 4. Order-to-Cash (O2C) Process
Understanding the complete sales cycle, starting from customer order creation to delivery, billing and payment processing.

### 5. Pricing Configuration
Understanding how pricing conditions, discounts and other pricing elements are used in sales transactions.

### 6. Delivery and Shipping
Learning how delivery documents are created and how shipping activities are managed.

### 7. Billing Process
Understanding how billing documents are generated after the delivery process.

### 8. SAP SD and FI Integration
Exploring how sales and billing activities are connected with financial accounting.

### 9. Configuration and Customization
Understanding how configuration parameters and system enhancements can be considered to meet specific business requirements.
